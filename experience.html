Experience

Health data work across research, hospitals and health systems.

Five roles across the UK and Nigeria, each closer to the point where analysis changes a decision.

June to August 2026NHS EnglandHealthcare Public Health, North West

Data Analyst (Analytical Intern)

Selected for NHS England's Early Talent programme. Owned a regional analysis of cardiovascular, kidney and metabolic (CVKM) disease in A&E, from a short brief to scope, cohort definition, pipeline, outputs and presentations.

  • Built a reusable SQL pipeline in Databricks (NHS UDAL) over the Emergency Care Data Set: 3,978,791 North West A&E attendances, 2 foundation views and 13 steps that regenerate in about 36 seconds.
  • Built a 607-code SNOMED CT definition from 13 seed concepts, checked it against the national specification (45 redundant, 11 missing codes) and agreed it with an A&E consultant and a national GP lead.
  • Found 41% of attendances had no diagnosis code and reported on two denominators, with a coding coverage table by ICB, so low counts in poorly recording areas read as under-recording, not lower need.
  • Automated a 27-slide pack in Python, presented to senior leaders and live from Tableau to leads from three ICBs. The work now supports an ICB commissioning proposal.
  • Ran a weekly meeting across four stakeholder groups, absorbed a mid-project scope change without moving the deadline, and was featured in the NHS England North West Weekly Bulletin.
