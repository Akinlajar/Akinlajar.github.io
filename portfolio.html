NHS Operations

Recovering lost NHS capacity: missed appointment prediction

Analysed 866 million NHS GP appointment records. 40.6 million were missed (4.42%), about £1.22 billion a year. Video appointments were missed 12 times less often than face-to-face (0.46% vs 5.56%), and the analysis ended in sized, costed recommendations rather than a descriptive report.

SQL, Python, pandas, scikit-learn
