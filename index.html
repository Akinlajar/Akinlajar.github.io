About

Clinical instinct.
Technical toolkit.

I'm a data scientist and ML engineer who works on healthcare data problems end to end, from the pipeline that makes data trustworthy to the model that acts on it.

I trained in clinical sciences, then in health data science, so I understand what the variables mean, not just how to model them. I've worked with hospitals, research labs, businesses and the NHS.
